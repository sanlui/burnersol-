Why clean your Phantom wallet

Phantom is one of the most popular Solana wallets, but over time it accumulates empty token accounts, spam airdrops, and unused NFTs. Each empty SPL token account locks approximately 0.00203928 SOL in rent that could be returned to your main balance.

Regular cleanup reduces network clutter, lowers the risk of interacting with malicious spam tokens, and reclaims SOL that is otherwise trapped in dormant accounts. A clean Phantom wallet also makes it easier to manage the tokens you actually use.

Step-by-step guide for Phantom cleanup

BurnerSOL integrates directly with Phantom's wallet adapter. Follow these steps to clean your wallet:

  • Open BurnerSOL and click the connect button to link your Phantom wallet.
  • Allow BurnerSOL to read your token accounts. No transactions are signed at this stage.
  • Review the list of accounts displayed. Each entry shows the token name, balance, and whether the account is empty or has a balance you may want to keep.
  • Select the accounts you want to close. BurnerSOL groups empty accounts for bulk cleanup.
  • Preview the total SOL rent recovery before approving. Phantom will show you the exact transaction details.
  • Sign the transaction in Phantom only after confirming the amounts match your expectations.

Security considerations for Phantom users

Phantom wallet users should be aware of several security practices when using cleanup tools:

  • Always verify the URL is burnersol.io before connecting. Phishing sites may impersonate cleanup tools.
  • Never share your Phantom seed phrase with any website or tool. BurnerSOL never asks for seed phrases.
  • Review each transaction Phantom displays before signing. Check the instruction type and the accounts involved.
  • Start with a small test cleanup if you are using BurnerSOL for the first time to verify the process works as expected.
  • Keep Phantom updated to the latest version for the most current security patches and wallet features.

What types of accounts can be cleaned

BurnerSOL can help you clean several types of accounts from your Phantom wallet:

  • Empty SPL token accounts — Accounts with zero balance that still hold rent deposits.
  • Spam airdropped tokens — Unwanted tokens sent to your wallet by unknown projects.
  • Unwanted NFTs — NFTs you no longer want to hold in your wallet.
  • LP token accounts — Liquidity pool tokens from completed or exited positions.
  • Token accounts from sold tokens — Leftover accounts after selling or transferring tokens.

How do I clean up my Phantom wallet?

Connect your Phantom wallet to BurnerSOL, review all token accounts and their status, select empty or unwanted accounts, and sign only the cleanup transactions you approve. BurnerSOL never takes custody of your assets.

Is it safe to use BurnerSOL with Phantom?

Yes, BurnerSOL is a non-custodial tool that works directly with Phantom's wallet interface. You review every transaction before signing, and Phantom shows you exactly what you are approving. Your private keys never leave your Phantom wallet.

What accounts can I clean from my Phantom wallet?

You can close empty SPL token accounts, burn unwanted tokens or NFTs, and recover SOL rent from accounts you no longer need. This includes airdropped spam tokens and accounts from tokens you sold or transferred.

How much SOL can I recover by cleaning my Phantom wallet?

The amount depends on how many empty token accounts you have. Each empty SPL token account holds approximately 0.00203928 SOL in rent. Users with many airdropped tokens can recover significant amounts over time.

Will cleaning affect my Phantom wallet balance or tokens?

Only accounts you explicitly select for cleanup will be affected. Your main SOL balance, active tokens, and NFTs remain untouched. You preview the exact outcome before approving any transaction in Phantom.
