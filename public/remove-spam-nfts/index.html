Spam NFT Removal — Frequently Asked Questions

Solana spam NFT questions answered

What are spam NFTs on Solana?

Spam NFTs are unwanted or unsolicited NFTs sent to your Solana wallet, often as part of phishing or airdrop scams. They clutter your wallet, waste screen space, and can be risky if you interact with links or signatures associated with them.

How do I remove spam NFTs from my wallet?

Connect your wallet to BurnerSOL, review the NFTs in your wallet, select the spam NFTs you want to remove, and sign the transaction to burn or transfer them out. The process is straightforward and secure.

Is it safe to burn NFTs on Solana?

Yes. Burning an NFT removes it permanently from your wallet and returns any locked rent. Only burn NFTs you are certain are spam or unwanted. BurnerSOL lets you review every detail before signing.

Can I recover SOL from spam NFT accounts?

Yes. When you burn or remove an NFT and close the associated token account, the rent deposit is returned to your wallet. This helps you recover SOL while cleaning up your wallet from spam.

Should I interact with unknown NFTs in my wallet?

No. Unknown or unsolicited NFTs can be phishing attempts designed to trick you into signing malicious transactions. Never click links or sign transactions related to unfamiliar NFTs. Use a trusted tool like BurnerSOL to safely remove them.
