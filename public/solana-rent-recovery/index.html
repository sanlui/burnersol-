Solana Rent Recovery — Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding rent on the Solana blockchain

What is Solana rent and why is it locked?

Solana requires a minimum SOL deposit (rent) to keep an account alive on-chain. When a token account becomes empty, the rent remains locked until the account is closed. BurnerSOL helps you close these accounts and recover the rent that is sitting idle.

How does Solana rent recovery work?

BurnerSOL scans your wallet for empty token accounts, shows you how much rent is locked in each, and lets you close them in a batched transaction. The rent is returned to your wallet immediately upon confirmation by the Solana network.

How much rent can I recover?

Each empty token account holds approximately 0.00203 SOL. Active wallets with many accounts from trading or DeFi activity can recover several SOL total through systematic rent recovery.

Is Solana rent recovery safe?

Yes. BurnerSOL is non-custodial and only closes empty accounts with a zero balance. Your tokens, NFTs, and other assets are never touched. You review every action before signing the transaction.

Can I recover rent from all types of Solana accounts?

BurnerSOL focuses on SPL token accounts with a zero balance. Other account types like token metadata or program-owned accounts may have different rules. BurnerSOL identifies and handles the most common rent recovery scenarios.
