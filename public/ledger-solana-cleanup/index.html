Why clean your Ledger Solana wallet

Ledger hardware wallets provide the highest level of security for Solana, but they still accumulate empty token accounts over time. Each empty SPL token account locks approximately 0.00203928 SOL in rent that could be recovered to your main balance.

Cleaning your Ledger Solana wallet reduces on-chain clutter, minimizes exposure to potentially malicious spam tokens, and reclaims SOL that is otherwise trapped in dormant accounts. The hardware wallet's secure element ensures your keys remain protected throughout the process.

Step-by-step guide for Ledger Solana cleanup

BurnerSOL works with Ledger Live's Solana app to provide a secure cleanup experience:

  • Connect your Ledger device and open the Solana app. Make sure Ledger Live is running and your device is unlocked.
  • Visit BurnerSOL and click the connect button. Select Ledger as your wallet option.
  • Authorize BurnerSOL to read your token accounts. No transactions are signed at this stage.
  • Review the account list. Each entry shows the token name, balance, and whether the account is empty.
  • Select empty or unwanted accounts for cleanup. BurnerSOL groups accounts for efficient bulk processing.
  • Preview the total SOL recovery. Your Ledger device will display the transaction details for verification.
  • Physically confirm the transaction on your Ledger device only after verifying the amounts are correct.

Security considerations for Ledger users

Ledger hardware wallet users benefit from inherent security advantages, but should still follow best practices:

  • Always verify the URL is burnersol.io before connecting. Bookmark the site to avoid phishing attempts.
  • Your Ledger seed phrase should never be entered on any website. BurnerSOL does not request seed phrases.
  • All cleanup transactions require physical confirmation on your Ledger device, providing hardware-level security.
  • Review the transaction details displayed on your Ledger screen before pressing the confirm button.
  • Keep your Ledger firmware and Solana app updated to the latest versions for security improvements.
  • Consider using a dedicated Solana account on your Ledger for dApp interactions separate from your main holdings.

What types of accounts can be cleaned

BurnerSOL supports cleaning several account types from your Ledger Solana wallet:

  • Empty SPL token accounts — Zero-balance accounts that still consume rent deposits.
  • Spam airdropped tokens — Unsolicited tokens sent by unknown or suspicious projects.
  • Unwanted NFTs — NFTs you no longer wish to hold in your Ledger wallet.
  • LP token accounts — Liquidity pool tokens from closed or expired positions.
  • Token accounts from sold tokens — Residual accounts after selling or transferring tokens out.
