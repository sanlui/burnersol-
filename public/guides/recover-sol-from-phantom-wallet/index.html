Recover SOL from Phantom Wallet

Phantom is the most popular Solana wallet, and over time it accumulates empty token accounts that lock your SOL. This guide walks you through the process of recovering that locked SOL using BurnerSOL with your Phantom wallet.

Why Phantom Wallets Accumulate Locked SOL

Every time you receive a new token in Phantom — whether from an airdrop, a trade on Raydium or Jupiter, or an NFT mint — a new token account is created in your wallet. Each account locks approximately 0.00203928 SOL as rent exemption.

Phantom does not automatically close these accounts when you sell or transfer the tokens. Over months of active use, you may have dozens or even hundreds of empty token accounts, each holding a small amount of SOL that could be recovered.

Step-by-Step: Recover SOL with Phantom + BurnerSOL

Step 1: Open BurnerSOL

Navigate to burnersol.io in your desktop browser. Make sure you are on the official site. Bookmark it to avoid phishing sites.

Step 2: Connect Phantom Wallet

Click the connect button on BurnerSOL. Phantom will pop up asking for permission to connect. Review the connection request — BurnerSOL only needs read access to your accounts. Approve the connection.

Step 3: Review Your Token Accounts

BurnerSOL scans your Phantom wallet and displays all token accounts. You will see:

  • Accounts with tokens (these should NOT be closed).
  • Accounts with a zero balance (these are candidates for closure).
  • The rent amount locked in each account.
  • The total recoverable SOL if you close all eligible accounts.

Step 4: Select Empty Accounts

Use the "Select All Empty" button to mark all zero-balance accounts for closure. You can also manually select or deselect specific accounts if you want to keep some for future use.

Step 5: Close the Accounts

Click the close button. Phantom will display a transaction for you to review. The transaction shows:

  • Which accounts are being closed.
  • The total SOL being returned to your wallet.
  • The transaction fee (approximately 0.000005 SOL).

Review the details carefully, then click approve. The transaction is submitted to the Solana network and typically confirms within 1-2 seconds.

Step 6: Verify Your Balance

Open your Phantom wallet and check your SOL balance. You should see the recovered amount added to your balance. The empty accounts are no longer in your wallet.

Tips for Phantom Users

  • Use desktop for cleanup: BurnerSOL works best with the Phantom browser extension on desktop. Phantom mobile has limited dApp support.
  • Check periodically: Make wallet cleanup a regular habit — monthly for active users, quarterly for casual users.
  • Keep your Phantom updated: Use the latest version of Phantom for the best security and compatibility.
  • Verify the site: Always ensure you are on burnersol.io before connecting your wallet.

What If I Accidentally Closed an Account with Tokens?

If you close an account that still holds tokens, the tokens are not lost — they remain on the account address but become unassociated from your wallet. You can re-derive the associated token account to recover them. However, it is always best to verify that accounts have a zero balance before closing.

BurnerSOL clearly labels which accounts have tokens and which are empty, minimizing the risk of accidental closure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much SOL can I recover from Phantom wallet?

It depends on how many empty token accounts you have. Each account returns approximately 0.002 SOL. If you have 50 empty accounts, you can recover about 0.1 SOL.

Is BurnerSOL compatible with Phantom wallet?

Yes. BurnerSOL fully supports Phantom wallet. You can connect your Phantom wallet directly to BurnerSOL to scan and close empty token accounts.

Will closing accounts affect my Phantom wallet balance?

Closing empty accounts will increase your available SOL balance because the rent exemption deposits are returned. Your token balances are not affected.

Can I use BurnerSOL on Phantom mobile?

BurnerSOL works best on desktop with the Phantom browser extension. Phantom mobile has limited support for third-party dApps. Use a desktop browser for the best experience.

Do I need to disconnect from other dApps to use BurnerSOL?

No. You can use BurnerSOL alongside other connected dApps. Simply navigate to burnersol.io and connect your Phantom wallet. No disconnection from other sites is required.
