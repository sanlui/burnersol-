Token Burning — Frequently Asked Questions

Burning SPL tokens on Solana

What does it mean to burn tokens on Solana?

Burning tokens on Solana means permanently removing them from circulation by sending them to a burn address or closing the token account. This removes the tokens from your wallet and can reclaim the locked SOL rent deposit.

Why should I burn unwanted tokens?

Unwanted or spam tokens can clutter your wallet, waste rent deposits on their accounts, and pose security risks if they are part of phishing schemes. Burning them keeps your wallet clean and recovers locked SOL.

How do I burn tokens with BurnerSOL?

Connect your wallet to BurnerSOL, review the tokens in your wallet, select the ones you want to burn, and sign the transaction. BurnerSOL handles the burn and closes the token account to recover rent.

Can I burn tokens that have a small balance?

Yes. Any SPL token with a balance can be burned, regardless of the amount. BurnerSOL processes the burn and closes the associated token account, returning the rent to your wallet.

Is burning tokens on Solana safe?

Yes, when done through a trusted tool like BurnerSOL. Burning is a standard Solana operation that permanently removes tokens. Always review what you are burning before signing any transaction to ensure you are removing only unwanted tokens.
