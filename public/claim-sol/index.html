Claim SOL — Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about claiming SOL on Solana

How do I claim SOL from my Solana wallet?

Connect your wallet to BurnerSOL, let it scan for empty token accounts, review the total SOL available to claim, and sign the transaction. Your SOL is returned instantly to your wallet.

What is SOL rent on Solana?

SOL rent is a deposit required by the Solana network to keep an account alive on-chain. When you close an empty token account, this rent deposit is returned to your wallet as a refund. It is not a fee — it is your SOL being unlocked.

Can I claim SOL from tokens I no longer hold?

Yes. If you received tokens in the past but sold or transferred them, the empty token account still exists on-chain and holds rent. BurnerSOL lets you close that account and claim the SOL locked inside it.

Is there a limit to how much SOL I can claim?

There is no limit. You can claim all recoverable SOL from every eligible empty token account in your wallet. BurnerSOL batches them into a single transaction for efficiency.

Does claiming SOL affect my other tokens?

No. BurnerSOL only closes empty token accounts with a zero balance. Your other tokens, NFTs, staked positions, and wallet balances remain completely untouched and unaffected.
