How to Remove Spam NFTs from Your Solana Wallet

Spam NFTs are one of the most common nuisances on Solana. They appear uninvited in your wallet, clutter your token list, and can pose security risks if you interact with them. This guide explains how to identify, avoid, and permanently remove spam NFTs from your wallet.

What Are Spam NFTs?

Spam NFTs are non-fungible tokens that are sent to your wallet address without your consent. On Solana, anyone can send tokens to any address — no approval is needed. Scammers exploit this to:

  • Send NFTs with misleading names like "Airdrop claim" or "Free SOL reward" to trick you into clicking malicious links.
  • Clutter your wallet with thousands of worthless NFTs, making it harder to manage your real assets.
  • Create a false sense of value — you might think you received something valuable when it is actually worthless.

The key safety rule: never interact with spam NFTs. Do not click links in their metadata, do not try to sell them on unfamiliar marketplaces, and do not sign any transaction prompted by the NFT.

Why Spam NFTs Are a Problem

Wallet Clutter

Spam NFTs make your wallet hard to navigate. If you have hundreds of spam NFTs mixed with your real collectibles, it becomes difficult to find and manage the ones you actually care about.

Security Risk

While receiving a spam NFT does not compromise your wallet, the NFT itself may contain malicious instructions in its metadata. If you try to list, sell, or transfer it using a less secure tool, you could inadvertently sign a harmful transaction. The safest action is to burn the NFT without interacting with its content.

Locked Rent

Each NFT you hold occupies a token account on-chain. Even if the NFT is worthless, it locks approximately 0.002 SOL in rent exemption. Hundreds of spam NFTs can lock significant amounts of SOL.

How to Remove Spam NFTs: Step-by-Step

Step 1: Identify the Spam NFTs

Open your wallet and review your NFT collection. Spam NFTs typically have characteristics like:

  • Names you do not recognize or that seem promotional ("Free reward," "Claim now").
  • No clear artwork or placeholder images.
  • Collections you never purchased or minted from.

Step 2: Use BurnerSOL to Burn Them

Go to burnersol.io and connect your wallet. BurnerSOL will display all your NFTs and token accounts. Select the spam NFTs you want to remove and use the burn function. The NFT is permanently destroyed and the rent is returned to your wallet.

Step 3: Close the Empty Accounts

After burning the NFTs, the associated token accounts become empty. You can close these accounts in the same session to recover the additional rent. BurnerSOL handles both steps — burning and account closure — in a streamlined workflow.

Step 4: Avoid Future Spam

You cannot completely prevent spam NFTs on Solana, but you can:

  • Keep your main wallet address private and use separate wallets for public interactions.
  • Use a "burner" wallet for minting and airdrop claims.
  • Periodically check and clean your wallet using BurnerSOL.

Safety Tips When Handling Spam NFTs

  • Do not click links in NFT descriptions or metadata.
  • Do not sign transactions prompted by unknown NFTs.
  • Do not try to sell spam NFTs on unverified marketplaces.
  • Burn them directly using a trusted, non-custodial tool.
  • Verify the transaction details in your wallet popup before signing any burn transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are spam NFTs on Solana?

Spam NFTs are unsolicited NFTs that appear in your wallet without your consent. They are typically sent by scammers who hope you will interact with them, visit a malicious link, or sign a dangerous transaction.

Can spam NFTs harm my wallet?

Simply receiving a spam NFT does not harm your wallet. However, interacting with it — such as clicking links in the metadata or trying to sell it on unknown marketplaces — can lead to wallet compromise. The safest approach is to burn them without interaction.

How do I burn an NFT on Solana?

You can burn an NFT by sending it to the Solana burn address or by using a tool like BurnerSOL that handles the burn process for you. Burning permanently removes the NFT from circulation and returns the rent.

Will burning an NFT recover rent?

Yes. When you burn an NFT, the account is closed and the rent exemption deposit (approximately 0.002 SOL) is returned to your wallet.

How can I prevent receiving spam NFTs?

There is no way to completely prevent spam NFTs on Solana since anyone can send tokens to any address. However, you can periodically clean your wallet using BurnerSOL and avoid interacting with suspicious NFTs.
