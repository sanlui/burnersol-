Wallet Rent Recovery — Frequently Asked Questions

Recovering rent from your Solana wallet

What is wallet rent on Solana?

Wallet rent on Solana refers to the SOL deposits required to maintain token accounts in your wallet. Each empty account locks approximately 0.00203 SOL that can be recovered by closing the account through a tool like BurnerSOL.

How do I recover rent from my Solana wallet?

Connect your wallet to BurnerSOL, let it scan for empty token accounts, review the rent amounts, and sign the transaction. BurnerSOL closes the accounts and returns the rent to your wallet immediately.

How much wallet rent can I recover?

The amount depends on how many empty token accounts you have. Each account holds about 0.00203 SOL in rent. Users with many accounts from trading or DeFi activity can recover significant amounts over time.

Will recovering wallet rent affect my tokens?

No. BurnerSOL only closes accounts with a zero balance. Your tokens, NFTs, staked positions, and other assets are never touched during the wallet rent recovery process.

Is wallet rent recovery a one-time process?

You can recover wallet rent anytime. As you interact with new tokens, empty accounts may accumulate again. Many users recover rent periodically to keep their wallets organized and their SOL liquid.
