Close Token Accounts — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about closing Solana token accounts

How do I close empty token accounts on Solana?

Connect your wallet to BurnerSOL, scan for empty token accounts, review the list and rent amounts, and sign the batch-close transaction. BurnerSOL handles the rest and returns the rent to your wallet.

What happens when I close a token account?

Closing a token account removes it from the Solana blockchain and returns the rent deposit to your wallet. If you need that token later, a new account will be created automatically when you receive it again.

Can I close token accounts that held NFTs?

Yes, if the NFT has been transferred out and the account has a zero balance, it can be closed. BurnerSOL identifies all zero-balance accounts regardless of what token or NFT they previously held.

How many token accounts can I close at once?

BurnerSOL batches all eligible accounts into a single transaction. You can close dozens or even hundreds of empty accounts in one step, minimizing network fees and saving time.

Will closing token accounts affect my wallet balance?

Closing empty accounts increases your wallet's SOL balance by returning the rent deposits. It does not affect your other tokens, NFTs, staked positions, or any other assets in your wallet.
