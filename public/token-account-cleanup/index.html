Token Account Cleanup — Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding SPL token account cleanup

What is token account cleanup on Solana?

Token account cleanup is the process of identifying and closing empty SPL token accounts in your Solana wallet to recover the SOL rent deposits locked in those accounts. It helps you reclaim value and keep your wallet organized.

Why do I have so many empty token accounts?

Every time you receive a new SPL token, a token account is created in your wallet. If you sell, swap, or transfer that token, the account remains with a zero balance, locking up rent. Frequent trading and DeFi activity accumulate these accounts quickly.

How much SOL is locked in empty token accounts?

Each empty SPL token account holds approximately 0.00203 SOL for rent. With dozens or hundreds of empty accounts from trading activity, this can add up to several SOL total that you can recover through cleanup.

Can I clean up token accounts without losing my tokens?

Yes. BurnerSOL only targets accounts with a zero balance. Your tokens, NFTs, and other assets are never affected by the cleanup process. Only empty accounts are closed and their rent is returned to your wallet.

How often should I do token account cleanup?

It depends on your Solana activity. Frequent traders or DeFi users may want to clean up monthly to keep rent costs down. Casual users can do it quarterly or whenever they notice their wallet accumulating empty accounts.
