Recover SOL — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about recovering SOL on Solana

How do I recover SOL from empty token accounts?

Connect your Solana wallet to BurnerSOL, scan for empty token accounts, review the rent that can be recovered, and sign the close instruction to get your SOL back. The process takes seconds and the SOL is returned to your wallet immediately.

Is it safe to recover SOL using BurnerSOL?

Yes. BurnerSOL is non-custodial. It never holds your private keys. You review every instruction before signing, and only the exact close-account transaction is sent. No other wallet actions are performed without your explicit approval.

How much SOL can I recover from empty accounts?

Each token account on Solana reserves approximately 0.00203 SOL for rent. If you have dozens or hundreds of empty accounts, the total recovered SOL adds up quickly. Some users recover several SOL from accumulated empty accounts.

Will recovering SOL close my token accounts permanently?

Yes. Closing an empty token account permanently removes it and returns the rent to your wallet. If you need that token again later, a new account can be created by simply receiving that token again.

Can I recover SOL from accounts with zero balance but non-zero mint?

Yes. Token accounts with a zero balance can be closed even if the associated token mint still exists. The rent deposit is returned to your wallet upon closing. BurnerSOL identifies all such accounts automatically.
