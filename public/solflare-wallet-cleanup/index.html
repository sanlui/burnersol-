Why clean your Solflare wallet

Solflare is a feature-rich Solana wallet, but regular use leads to accumulation of empty token accounts, spam airdrops, and unused NFTs. Each empty SPL token account locks approximately 0.00203928 SOL in rent that could be returned to your main balance.

Cleaning your Solflare wallet reduces on-chain clutter, minimizes exposure to potentially malicious spam tokens, and reclaims SOL that is otherwise trapped in dormant accounts. A streamlined wallet also improves performance when loading your token list.

Step-by-step guide for Solflare cleanup

BurnerSOL works with Solflare's wallet adapter to provide a seamless cleanup experience:

  • Navigate to BurnerSOL and click the connect button to link your Solflare wallet.
  • Authorize BurnerSOL to read your token accounts. No transactions are signed during this step.
  • Review the account list. Each entry displays the token name, balance, and rent status.
  • Select empty or unwanted accounts for cleanup. Solflare groups accounts by type for easier selection.
  • Check the estimated SOL recovery total before proceeding. Solflare will display the full transaction details.
  • Sign the transaction in Solflare after confirming the amounts and instruction types are correct.

Security considerations for Solflare users

Solflare users should follow these security practices when performing wallet cleanup:

  • Verify the URL is burnersol.io before connecting. Bookmark the site to avoid phishing.
  • Never enter your Solflare seed phrase on any website. BurnerSOL does not require or request seed phrases.
  • Use Solflare's transaction simulation feature to preview the outcome before approving cleanup instructions.
  • Consider creating a separate Solflare wallet for experimental dApps and keep your main wallet for cleanup.
  • Enable Solflare's security alerts and review any warnings before signing transactions.

What types of accounts can be cleaned

BurnerSOL supports cleaning several account types from your Solflare wallet:

  • Empty SPL token accounts — Zero-balance accounts that still consume rent deposits.
  • Spam airdropped tokens — Unsolicited tokens sent by unknown or suspicious projects.
  • Unwanted NFTs — NFTs you no longer wish to hold in your Solflare wallet.
  • LP token accounts — Liquidity pool tokens from closed or expired positions.
  • Token accounts from sold tokens — Residual accounts after selling or transferring tokens out.

Solflare wallet FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Solflare wallet cleanup.

How do I clean up my Solflare wallet?

Connect your Solflare wallet to BurnerSOL, review all token accounts and their status, select empty or unwanted accounts, and sign only the cleanup transactions you approve. BurnerSOL never takes custody of your assets.

Is it safe to use BurnerSOL with Solflare?

Yes, BurnerSOL is a non-custodial tool that integrates with Solflare's wallet interface. You review every transaction before signing, and Solflare shows you exactly what you are approving. Your private keys never leave your Solflare wallet.

What accounts can I clean from my Solflare wallet?

You can close empty SPL token accounts, burn unwanted tokens or NFTs, and recover SOL rent from accounts you no longer need. This includes airdropped spam tokens and accounts from tokens you sold or transferred.

How much SOL can I recover by cleaning my Solflare wallet?

The amount depends on how many empty token accounts you have. Each empty SPL token account holds approximately 0.00203928 SOL in rent. Users with many airdropped tokens can recover significant amounts over time.

Does Solflare show cleanup transactions clearly?

Yes, Solflare displays all transaction details including instruction types, accounts involved, and SOL amounts before you sign. This transparency helps you verify exactly what BurnerSOL is doing with your wallet.
