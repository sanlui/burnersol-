Wallet Cleaner — Frequently Asked Questions

Solana wallet cleaning explained

What is a Solana wallet cleaner?

A Solana wallet cleaner is a tool that helps you close empty token accounts, remove unwanted tokens or NFTs, and reclaim the SOL rent locked in those accounts. It keeps your wallet organized and recovers stuck SOL.

How does BurnerSOL clean my wallet?

BurnerSOL scans your wallet for empty token accounts, shows you the recoverable rent, and lets you close them in a batched transaction. It also identifies spam NFTs and unwanted tokens for removal, all in one workflow.

Is the wallet cleaner safe to use?

Yes. BurnerSOL is non-custodial and never holds your private keys. You review every instruction before signing, and only the accounts you approve are closed. No other wallet actions are performed.

Will cleaning my wallet delete my tokens?

No. Only empty accounts with a zero balance are closed. Your tokens, NFTs, staked positions, and other assets remain untouched in your wallet throughout the entire cleaning process.

How often should I clean my Solana wallet?

It depends on your activity. If you frequently interact with new tokens, DeFi protocols, or airdrops, you may accumulate empty accounts. Cleaning periodically helps recover locked SOL and keeps your wallet tidy.
