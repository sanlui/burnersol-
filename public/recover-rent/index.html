Rent Recovery — Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding Solana rent and recovery

What is Solana rent recovery?

Solana rent recovery is the process of closing empty token accounts to reclaim the SOL deposit (rent) that was locked when those accounts were created. BurnerSOL makes this process fast, transparent, and safe.

How much rent can I recover from my Solana wallet?

Each empty token account holds approximately 0.00203 SOL in rent. If you have accumulated dozens or hundreds of empty accounts from trading, airdrops, or DeFi activity, the total recovered SOL can be significant.

Does rent recovery work for all Solana token accounts?

Any SPL token account with a zero balance can be closed to recover rent. Accounts that still hold tokens cannot be closed until the tokens are transferred or burned first. BurnerSOL only targets eligible accounts.

Is there a fee for rent recovery?

BurnerSOL charges no platform fee. You only pay the standard Solana network transaction fee, which is a tiny fraction of a cent, regardless of how many accounts you close in a single batch.

Can I recover rent from accounts created by DeFi protocols?

Yes. If a DeFi protocol created token accounts in your wallet that are now empty, you can close them and recover the rent. BurnerSOL identifies all eligible accounts regardless of their origin or creator.
