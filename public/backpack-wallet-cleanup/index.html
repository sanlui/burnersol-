Why clean your Backpack wallet

Backpack is a popular Solana wallet built by the Mad Lads community, but like any wallet it accumulates empty token accounts over time. Each empty SPL token account locks approximately 0.00203928 SOL in rent that could be recovered to your main balance.

Regular cleanup of your Backpack wallet reduces network clutter, lowers the risk of interacting with malicious spam tokens, and frees up SOL that is otherwise trapped in dormant accounts. A clean wallet also makes it easier to find the tokens and NFTs you actually care about.

Step-by-step guide for Backpack cleanup

BurnerSOL integrates with Backpack's wallet adapter for a straightforward cleanup process:

  • Visit BurnerSOL and click the connect button to link your Backpack wallet.
  • Allow BurnerSOL to read your token accounts. No transactions are signed at this stage.
  • Review the displayed accounts. Each entry shows the token name, balance, and whether the account is empty.
  • Select the accounts you want to close. BurnerSOL groups empty accounts for efficient bulk cleanup.
  • Preview the total SOL rent recovery before approving. Backpack will show you the exact transaction details.
  • Sign the transaction in Backpack only after confirming the amounts match your expectations.

Security considerations for Backpack users

Backpack users should follow these security practices when using cleanup tools:

  • Always verify the URL is burnersol.io before connecting. Phishing sites may impersonate cleanup tools.
  • Never share your Backpack seed phrase with any website or tool. BurnerSOL never asks for seed phrases.
  • Review each transaction Backpack displays before signing. Check the instruction type and the accounts involved.
  • Backpack's transaction simulation can help you verify the expected outcome before approving cleanup instructions.
  • Keep your Backpack extension or app updated to the latest version for security improvements.

What types of accounts can be cleaned

BurnerSOL can help you clean several types of accounts from your Backpack wallet:

  • Empty SPL token accounts — Accounts with zero balance that still hold rent deposits.
  • Spam airdropped tokens — Unwanted tokens sent to your wallet by unknown projects.
  • Unwanted NFTs — NFTs you no longer want to hold in your Backpack wallet.
  • LP token accounts — Liquidity pool tokens from completed or exited positions.
  • Token accounts from sold tokens — Leftover accounts after selling or transferring tokens.

How do I clean up my Backpack wallet?

Connect your Backpack wallet to BurnerSOL, review all token accounts and their status, select empty or unwanted accounts, and sign only the cleanup transactions you approve. BurnerSOL never takes custody of your assets.

Is it safe to use BurnerSOL with Backpack?

Yes, BurnerSOL is a non-custodial tool that works with Backpack's wallet interface. You review every transaction before signing, and Backpack shows you exactly what you are approving. Your private keys never leave your Backpack wallet.

What accounts can I clean from my Backpack wallet?

You can close empty SPL token accounts, burn unwanted tokens or NFTs, and recover SOL rent from accounts you no longer need. This includes airdropped spam tokens and accounts from tokens you sold or transferred.

How much SOL can I recover by cleaning my Backpack wallet?

The amount depends on how many empty token accounts you have. Each empty SPL token account holds approximately 0.00203928 SOL in rent. Users with many airdropped tokens can recover significant amounts over time.

Can I use BurnerSOL with Backpack on mobile?

Yes, BurnerSOL works with Backpack on both desktop and mobile. The Backpack mobile app supports WalletConnect, allowing you to connect and approve cleanup transactions from your phone.
