How to Close Token-2022 Accounts on Solana

Token-2022 is the next-generation token program on Solana, offering powerful features like transfer fees, permanent delegates, and non-transferable tokens. But these advanced features come with larger account sizes and higher rent costs. This guide explains how to close Token-2022 accounts and recover your SOL.

What Is Token-2022?

Token-2022 (also known as Token Extensions) is a newer token program on Solana that extends the original SPL Token standard. It supports additional features called extensions, which include:

  • Transfer fees: Automatic fee collection on every token transfer.
  • Permanent delegate: A designated authority that can transfer or burn tokens from any account.
  • Non-transferable tokens: Tokens that cannot be moved between wallets (soulbound tokens).
  • Confidential transfers: Hidden transfer amounts for privacy.
  • Interest-bearing tokens: Tokens that accrue interest over time.

Many new token issuers are choosing Token-2022 for its flexibility. As a result, users are increasingly interacting with Token-2022 accounts alongside traditional SPL accounts.

Key Differences from SPL Token Accounts

Account Size

Standard SPL token accounts are 165 bytes. Token-2022 accounts can be significantly larger — ranging from 182 bytes to several hundred bytes — depending on which extensions are enabled. Larger accounts require more rent exemption.

Rent Cost

Because Token-2022 accounts are larger, the rent exemption deposit is higher. While a standard SPL token account locks approximately 0.002 SOL, a Token-2022 account might lock 0.003 to 0.005+ SOL depending on its configuration.

Program Association

SPL token accounts are owned by the Token Program (TokenkegQfeZyiNwAJbNbGKPFXCWuBvf9Ss623VQ5DA). Token-2022 accounts are owned by the Token-2022 Program (TokenzQdBNbLqP5VEhdkAS6EPFLC1PHnBqCXEpPxuEb). This distinction matters when constructing close transactions.

How to Close Token-2022 Accounts

Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

Visit burnersol.io and connect your Solana wallet. BurnerSOL automatically detects both SPL Token and Token-2022 accounts in your wallet.

Step 2: Identify Empty Token-2022 Accounts

BurnerSOL labels each account with its program type. Look for Token-2022 accounts that have a zero balance. These are candidates for closure.

Step 3: Select and Close

Select the empty Token-2022 accounts you want to close. Click the close button and review the transaction in your wallet. The transaction uses the Token-2022 program's close_account instruction, which is different from the standard SPL close instruction.

Step 4: Confirm and Recover

Sign the transaction. The rent is returned to your wallet and the accounts are permanently removed from the chain. Verify your balance to confirm the recovery.

Why Token-2022 Accounts Matter for Cleanup

Because Token-2022 accounts lock more rent per account, cleaning them up provides a greater SOL recovery per closure. If you have been active with newer DeFi protocols or tokens that use Token-2022, you may have several accounts locking 0.003+ SOL each.

For reference, 50 empty Token-2022 accounts could lock approximately 0.15 to 0.25 SOL — a meaningful amount that could be put to better use.

Security Considerations

Closing Token-2022 accounts follows the same security principles as closing SPL accounts:

  • Only close accounts with a zero balance.
  • Verify the transaction details before signing.
  • Use a non-custodial tool like BurnerSOL that does not require seed phrase entry.
  • Be aware that Token-2022 accounts with extensions like permanent delegate may have additional closure requirements.
